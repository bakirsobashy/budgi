# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is handled when you use the Finance Tracker mobile application ("the App").

## Data controller

The App is a personal, offline-first finance tracker developed and published by the individual developer listed in the app's store listing and in-app "About" dialog.

## Information we collect

**We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal or financial information on our servers.**

All data you enter into the App — including transactions, categories, budgets, and amounts — is stored locally on your device using an embedded SQLite database. The App does not require user accounts, does not connect to the internet to sync your data, and does not transmit your data to any external service.

## Permissions

The App may request only the minimum permissions necessary to run on your device. Currently, the App does not require network access to function, nor does it access your contacts, camera, microphone, files, or location for its core features.

## Third-party services

The App does not use third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or cloud-storage services. No third parties have access to your data through the App.

## Data security

Your data is stored locally in the App's private storage on your device. It is protected by the standard Android sandbox and any device-level encryption you have enabled. Because your data never leaves your device, it is not accessible to us.

## Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you choose to delete it. You can remove individual transactions, categories, or budgets within the App, or clear all data from the App in your device settings. Uninstalling the App will remove all locally stored data.

## External links

The App may contain a link to an external donation page. Opening that link uses your device's default browser. We do not control or collect any information from those external sites.

## Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in the App and updated with a new "Last updated" date.

## Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the App's data practices, please contact the developer through the email address shown in the in-app "About" dialog or the store listing.
